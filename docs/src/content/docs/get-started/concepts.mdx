**Cluster.** A Calimero 1.0 deployment is a cluster of `merod` nodes running in
Intel TDX. Each app instance is replicated by its own Raft log across them, so
any node answers for any app; `call` commits through the log, `view` reads
(strongly by default, or from any replica at least as new as `min_index` with
`stale=True`).

**Attestation.** Before trusting a node the client verifies its TDX quote. A
real quote is accepted only from an image named by a signed mero-tee release
under one of `tee_profiles` (`min_release` sets a floor; debug profiles are
refused unless `allow_debug_profiles=True`). Development clusters serve mock
quotes, accepted only with `allow_mock=True`. `app_hash` additionally pins the
bundle the quote must bind.

**Sealed channel.** Requests are encrypted to the attested node's key, so TLS
terminating at a proxy in front of the node sees nothing.

**Accounts and passkeys.** An account signs in with a WebAuthn passkey
(`sign_up`, `sign_in`, `add_passkey`). `SoftPasskey` is an in-memory one for
scripts and tests. Methods an app marks `confirm` need a fresh passkey assertion
over that exact call (`call_confirmed`); owner approvals use `post_confirmed`.

**API keys.** Services authenticate with `cal_...` keys (`create_api_key`,
`use_api_key`), optionally scoped to apps and methods, read-only, or time-limited.

**Apps.** An app is a WASM bundle (`upload_bundle`) instantiated with
`create_app`. Access is the owner's, an organization's, a team's, a list of
accounts', or public (`set_access`). `upgrade_app` moves it to another bundle
and plans the state migration from the two ABIs.

**Organizations and teams.** v1's namespaces are organizations and its groups are
teams. Organizations have members with roles (`guest`, `member`, `admin`,
`owner`); teams nest, are joined by invite or open to everyone who reaches the
parent, and hold apps.
